ImgBatch Support

Open a request on GitHub. We usually answer within a few days.

The Finder Quick Actions are missing

Open System Settings → Extensions → Finder and switch on “HEIC → JPG” and “ImgBatch…”. They are off until you enable them, like every Quick Action.

Nothing is saved next to my photos

Open ImgBatch and allow access to your Home folder once (Allow Access…). Without it, results go to the output folder you choose in Settings.

Only 10 images are converted

The free version converts up to 10 images per run. Pro removes the limit and adds WebP, AVIF, GIF, presets and full metadata removal.

Restore my Pro purchase

Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Поддержка

Пишите через GitHub. Быстрые действия включаются в Системных настройках → Расширения → Finder. Восстановить Pro: Настройки → Pro → «Восстановить покупки».
